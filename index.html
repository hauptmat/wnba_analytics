What is MVP Score?

MVP Score is a 0–100 rating designed to measure how closely a player's current-season profile matches the type of profile that has historically earned WNBA MVP voting support. The production model was built from official MVP voting results from 2018, 2019, and 2021–2025, then applied to current-season player and team data. It is intended to describe MVP-caliber performance, not simply identify the league leader in one statistic.

What goes into the score?

The current production model uses five components. Player performance is evaluated relative to the current eligible candidate pool, so the score reflects how strongly a player stands out from her competition in that season.

36.6% PIE Overall statistical impact
26.3% Scoring Points per game
17.5% Secondary strength Rebounding or assists, whichever is stronger
11.5% Team success Team winning percentage
8.1% Workload Total minutes played

What is Projected Vote Share?

Projected Vote Share is the model's estimate of how the available MVP ballot points would be distributed if the historical relationship between these inputs and MVP voting carried over to the current season. The percentages are normalized across eligible players to total 100%. It is a model output, not polling, a probability of winning, or an official WNBA vote count.

Reading the table: MVP Score summarizes the strength of a player's MVP profile on a 0–100 scale. MVP Rank is based on the model's projected ballot-point share, with MVP Score used as a tiebreaker.
